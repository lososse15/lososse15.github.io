Explain like I’m 5: This study looked at people whose brain or balance system makes walking harder. It suggests that practicing the right walking or balance tasks helps the body learn again. More good practice can help people move more safely.

How to apply: Incorporate rotator cuff/scapular strengthening and progressive exposure to reaching/lifting based on irritability.
